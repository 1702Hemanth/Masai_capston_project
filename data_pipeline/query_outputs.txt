Query 6 - Books with category names
======================================================================

SQL:
SELECT
                b.title,
                b.price_gbp,
                b.rating,
                c.category_name
            FROM books b
            JOIN categories c
                ON b.category_id = c.category_id
            ORDER BY b.price_gbp DESC
            LIMIT 15;
